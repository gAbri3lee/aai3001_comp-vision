    Trained on five Roboflow v4 classes: Escalator, People, Pillar, Stair and Wet Floor Sign. Detection is supplementary and can miss hazards; "No hazards" does not mean the scene is safe. Pillar is the weakest class and is often missed. Stair direction is unknown; a Wet Floor Sign detection does not measure floor wetness. Distance is an estimate from box size, not measured metres. Depth, when enabled, is relative, not metres. Far detections, side objects and low-confidence general detections are shown as ignored and are not spoken. Other common objects are labelled Object by a general detector; objects outside its coverage can still be missed. Cloud mode uploads camera frames.
