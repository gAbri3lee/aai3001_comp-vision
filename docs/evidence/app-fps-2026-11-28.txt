VisionAid app frame rate, 2026-11-28 (Windows 11 laptop, 16 logical CPUs, CPU only)

Method: src/webapp.py --source "demo/videos/Stairs and pedestrians - field sequence.mp4" --no-tts,
/status sampled every 0.25 s for 12 s after a 3 s warm-up; medians. "detector" = hazard model + general
COCO model per frame (status latency_ms). Same-setting repeats differ by up to ~3 FPS.

Hazard model alone (scripts/verify_detector.py --frames 100, golden images, 4 ONNX threads):
  FP32 ONNX  44.8 ms  22.2 FPS
  INT8 ONNX  26.0 ms  37.7 FPS
General COCO YOLOv8n alone (Ultralytics predict, 640 px; scripts/quantize_general_model.py):
  yolov8n.pt (PyTorch)  56.4 ms
  INT8 ONNX             34.7 ms   (1.63x)

Whole app, depth off:
  hazard FP32 ONNX + general .pt     10.2 FPS  (detector 94.5 ms)
  hazard INT8      + general .pt     12.4 / 13.1 / 14.6 / 15.4 FPS  (detector 61-77 ms)
  hazard INT8      + general INT8    19.8 / 20.2 FPS  (detector 46-47 ms)   <- current default
Whole app, depth on (MiDaS adds ~100 ms/frame), measured before the general INT8 change:
  hazard FP32 ONNX + general .pt      4.9 FPS
  hazard INT8      + general .pt      5.7 FPS

Depth every Nth frame (src/depth.py ThrottledDepth, webapp --depth-every), measured as throughput: 150 frames of
the same video through the app's per-frame work (SceneDetector with both INT8 models + decide() with depth),
frames / total time, after a 10-frame warm-up. (Sampling /status is unreliable here: frame times alternate
between depth and non-depth frames, and a median of instantaneous FPS picks one or the other.)
  depth off        17.7 FPS   56.7 ms/frame
  depth every 1     7.5 FPS  132.9 ms/frame   (the behaviour before --depth-every)
  depth every 2    11.2 FPS   89.2 ms/frame
  depth every 3    13.0 FPS   76.7 ms/frame   <- default; map at most 2 frames (~150 ms) old
