VisionAid Photo and Video Check

Upload a photo or a video and the model looks for stairs, escalators, wet-floor signs and people.

or drag and drop an image or an MP4/WebM video here, or paste an image from the clipboard

    Supplementary only. This is a student prototype. A clear result does not mean a scene is safe, and a missed hazard is possible. Never rely on it for navigation.

    Privacy. Your photo is sent to the detection function to be analysed, then discarded: it is not saved or logged. Photos are shrunk in your browser before sending. A video is never uploaded: it plays in your browser, and only small JPEG snapshots of single frames (about two per second) are sent, then discarded.

    Accuracy. The four-class model can miss hazards, particularly in crowded scenes. In a video the boxes trail the picture by the detector's round-trip time, and frames are skipped when the detector is busy.
