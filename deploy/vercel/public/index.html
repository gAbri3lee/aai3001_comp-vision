VisionAid Photo Check

Upload a photo and the model looks for stairs, escalators, wet-floor signs, pillars and people.

or drag and drop an image here, or paste one from the clipboard

    Supplementary only. This is a student prototype. A clear result does not mean a scene is safe, and a missed hazard is possible. Never rely on it for navigation.

    Privacy. Your photo is sent to the detection function to be analysed, then discarded: it is not saved or logged. Photos are shrunk in your browser before sending.

    Accuracy. YOLOv8n (ONNX) trained on 8,247 images; mAP50 0.658 on a held-out test set of 920 photos. Pillars are the weakest class (about 17% recall).
